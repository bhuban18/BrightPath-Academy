## Brand & Style

This design system expresses disciplined academic ambition, modern pedagogical authority, and institutional integrity. Designed specifically for ambitious students preparing for premier competitive examinations (JEE, NEET, and Boards) and discerning parents seeking proven academic outcomes, the visual atmosphere balances institutional dignity with high-energy motivation.

The interface adheres to a **Modern Scholastic** design movement:
- Crisp architectural hierarchy layered over high-contrast white and warm ivory foundations.
- Confident, structured typography communicating clarity, order, and high intellectual standards.
- Deliberate focal accents leveraging warm saffron-amber to propel admissions conversion and highlight milestone achievements.
- Strategic deployment of emerald accents denoting verified selections, top percentile ranks, and real-time counselor accessibility.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional gravitas with dynamic conversion and trust indicators:

- **Primary Navy (`#0F1E36`)**: Anchor shade representing discipline, institutional permanence, and analytical rigor. Applied to primary headings, authoritative banners, dark-mode navigational elements, and strong structural boundaries. Secondary Navy tone (`#1A2E4D`) provides surface variation for card headers, floating badges, and high-tier structural components.
- **Secondary Saffron / Amber (`#FF6B35`)**: The core energetic catalyst. Used for primary conversion triggers (Apply Now, Book Scholarship Test, Download Syllabus), countdown trackers, and high-impact stat figures. Secondary ramp extends to `#FA8A3C` for soft hover gradients and alert pills.
- **Tertiary Emerald (`#10B981`)**: The symbol of verified success and instant resolution. Dedicated to AIR (All India Rank) badges, qualifying status pills, success rates, and direct WhatsApp counseling entry points.
- **Neutrals & Canvases (`#F8FAFC` & `#FFFFFF`)**: Pure light environment ensuring maximum cognitive ease during long reading sessions of test series schedules, syllabus breakdowns, and fee matrices. Text hierarchy leverages slate neutrals (`#334155` for high-readability body, `#64748B` for meta captions, and `#E2E8F0` for structural separators).

## Typography

The type system pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** with **Inter** to project clarity, authority, and high legibility across dense curriculum guides and scorecards:

- **Headings & Metric Displays (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Contemporary geometric forms with generous counters and humanistic terminals. Conveys modern optimism without compromising institutional respect. Display styles use heavy weights (`700` and `800`) with tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to produce disciplined headline locks.
- **Reading Body & Administrative Meta (Inter)**: Renowned for neutral clarity at small-to-medium optical sizes. Governs course descriptions, faculty bios, test instructions, and parent-student dashboards.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Employs medium-to-bold weights with slight positive tracking to ensure instant scannability for course tags (e.g., `JEE ADVANCED 2026`, `HYBRID BATCH`, `CBSE CLASS XII`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an 8pt structural rhythm combined with a responsive 12-column grid:

- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column layout maxed at 1280px or 1400px wide. Outer container margin is generous (`margin-desktop`: `5rem`) with `1.5rem` gutters, establishing an uncrowded academic journal atmosphere.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` page margins. Card grids collapse from 3 or 4 columns to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Course comparison tables convert into stacked swipeable cards, and sticky bottom navigation/inquiry bars engage.
- **Vertical Spacing**: Section paddings leverage extended multiples of `space-xl` (`5rem` to `7.5rem`) on desktop to distinctly separate content domains (e.g., Faculty Credentials, Rank Highlights, Scholarship Tests).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Ambient Academic Elevation**—diffuse, low-contrast shadows combined with hairline borders to create subtle physical lift without visual clutter:

- **Base Card Elevation**: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.06)`. Paired with a delicate boundary of `border: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Creates a floating surface that feels architectural and crisp.
- **Interactive Card Hover**: Translates `-3px` on the Y-axis with an intensified lift: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.10)`, transitioning border tone to `rgba(255, 107, 53, 0.25)`.
- **Institutional Elevated Panels**: For rank showcases and fee breakdowns, stack layered backgrounds using pure `#FFFFFF` over `#F8FAFC`, relying on `0 4px 12px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.03)` for intermediate separation.
- **Overlays, Drawers & Modals**: `box-shadow: 0 25px 60px -15px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.25)` backed by a backdrop blur of `8px` tinted with `rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced, refined geometry categorized as **Level 2 (Rounded)**:

- **Standard Cards & Containers**: Use `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / `16px`), offering a welcoming, modern feel that softens technical academic content.
- **Action Buttons & Form Controls**: Standardized at `0.75rem` (`12px`) for confident, tactile touchpoints.
- **Badges, Rank Chips, and Ticker Pills**: Built using full pill geometries (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish dynamic metadata from rectangular structural containers.
- **Inner Imagery & Exam Score Callouts**: Subordinated inside parent cards with a synchronized radius of `0.75rem` (`12px`) to maintain harmonious concentric borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Admission / Test Registration)**: Solid saffron gradient background (`#FF6B35` transitioning to `#FA8A3C`), text in `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`, height `48px` (desktop) / `52px` (mobile touch). Shadow: `0 4px 14px rgba(255, 107, 53, 0.35)`. Hover states shift brightness and elevate slightly.
- **Secondary (Course Brochure / Faculty)**: `#FFFFFF` background with a crisp border (`1.5px solid #0F1E36`), navy text (`#0F1E36`), hover fills softly with `rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.04)`.
- **WhatsApp Inquiry CTA**: Emerald background (`#10B981`), white text, accompanied by the official WhatsApp icon. Displays prominently as a fixed trigger on mobile and an accessible utility action across all desktop headers.

### Badges & Rank Markers
- **All India Rank (AIR) Badges**: Deep Navy pill background with gold/amber text (`#F59E0B`) or emerald fill (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`) with deep green typography (`#047857`). Used to frame rank proofs (e.g., `AIR 24 - JEE ADVANCED`).
- **Batch Status Chips**: Outlined pill styles using neutral slate backgrounds, uppercase tracking, and color-coded status dots (e.g., green dot for `Admissions Open`, amber dot for `Last 5 Seats`).

### Cards
- **Course & Cohort Cards**: Pure white surface, `16px` border-radius, soft diffuse shadow (`0 10px 30px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.06)`), outlined by `1px solid #F1F5F9`. Header contains exam stream tags, body contains target years and syllabus highlights, footer anchors duration and registration actions.
- **Topper Testimonial Cards**: Features portrait frame with subtle emerald verify badge, student score breakdown, board percentage, and enrolled program meta.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F1E36`, corner radius `12px`, padding `14px 16px`. Active focus state triggers a `2px` ring in Deep Navy (`#0F1E36`) with soft ambient saffron outline hints for application error validations.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Precision controls sized at `20px` with `4px` radius for checkboxes and full circular bounds for radios. In checked states, solid `#0F1E36` fill with pure white icons.

### Inquiry & Lead Capture Triggers
- **Sticky Parent Help Bar**: Mobile bottom-docked panel displaying dual actions: "Call Academic Counselor" (Secondary style) and "Chat on WhatsApp" (Tertiary emerald style).
- **Scholarship Calculator Cards**: Compact interactive module offering instant fee waiver estimates based on previous board scores, framed with a subtle saffron accent border.
